Mix and mastering for Carlos Cutaia's twelve-song album. Cutaia played Hammond organ and piano in the legendary Pescado Rabioso alongside Luis Alberto Spinetta — his co-composition "Ámame Peteribí" (1973) was sampled by Eminem on Music to Be Murdered By (2020), earning him a writing credit on one of the year's most talked-about records. Working with him at a point in my career when I had already mixed and mastered a significant body of piano-led work, this felt like the right project at the right time.
