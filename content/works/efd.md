Recording and production for Ensamble Folklórico Digital — a collective bringing together some of the most interesting voices in Argentina's digital folklore movement, including members of RUMBO TUMBA, Villa Diamante, and Alejandro Lauphan. Part of the broader Buenos Aires scene that was redefining what traditional Argentine music could sound and feel like in the electronic age.
