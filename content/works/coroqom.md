Recording, mixing, and mastering for the TV broadcast of Coro Qom Chelaalapi — founded in 1962 in Resistencia, Chaco, the first indigenous choir established in Latin America. Their name means "Flock of Thrushes" in the Qom language. Designated Living Cultural Heritage by UNESCO and Official Choir of the Province of Chaco, the ensemble carries the mission of preserving and transmitting the songs and traditions of the Qom people across generations.
