Composer on Anahí Berneri's 2014 feature — one of two Latin American titles selected for the main competition at San Sebastián International Film Festival, where it received a nomination for the Golden Seashell, the festival's top prize. The film also competed at the Sur Awards, Argentina's national film prizes, receiving multiple nominations including Best Screenplay.
