Music composition and sound library for Iris — a 26-episode animated children's series (6-9) produced by El Perro en la Luna as an Argentina–Cuba co-production. Premiered on Pakapaka, Argentina's national public children's channel, in 2021. The show follows Los Cuánticos, microscopic beings whose lives are governed by quantum physics, in a colourful nano-world studied by a baffled scientist named Newton.
