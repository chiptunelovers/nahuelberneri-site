Mastering engineer for all fifteen tracks of this landmark digital folklore compilation, released on Discos Crack. Flora y Fauna brought together some of Argentina's most adventurous producers and musicians — Pedro Canale (Chancha Vía Circuito), Kaleema, Tremor, Klik & Frik and more — to reinterpret traditional Andean and Argentine folk music through electronics, samplers and synthesis. The result was described by Página 12 as "a map of exploration" of Argentina's then-emerging digital folklore scene. Curated by Carla Sanguineti and Diego Bulacio (Villa Diamante).
