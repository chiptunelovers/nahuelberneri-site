Produced Emerger, the debut album by Buenos Aires heavy metal band Against, which went on to win the Wacken Metal Battle Argentina — earning the band a coveted spot at Wacken Open Air 2017 in Germany, one of the world's largest and most celebrated heavy metal festivals. Against performed on the Headbangers Stage alongside national winners from over 25 countries.
