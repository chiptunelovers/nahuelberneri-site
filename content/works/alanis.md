Composition and musical production for Anahí Berneri's feature film — over 20 original songs woven through the soundtrack. One of the most personal scores I've written. The film went on to win the Silver Seashell for Best Director and Best Actress at San Sebastián International Film Festival, the Grand Coral First Prize at Havana Film Festival, and Best Actress at the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences of Argentina. I was personally nominated for the Silver Condor Award for Best Music — Argentina's equivalent of the BAFTAs.
