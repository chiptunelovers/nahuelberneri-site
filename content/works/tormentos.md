Recording and mixing on Ejecutan El Macabro Plan (Scatter Records, 2017) — the comeback LP from Buenos Aires' premier surf rock instrumental quartet, their first album in nearly a decade. Cited by Rolling Stone as the "improbable kings of Río de la Plata surf", The Tormentos have spent 20+ years touring Europe, releasing music across Latin America, and collaborating with international surf icons including The Phantom Surfers, Los Straitjackets, and Davie Allan. Twelve tracks of pure fuzz and reverb.
