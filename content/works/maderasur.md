Madera Sur is one of the records I'm most proud of as an engineer. Recorded, mixed, and mastered at Camarón Brujo (Buenos Aires) over April and May 2018, it captures Facundo Salgado — aka Rumbo Tumba — and his one-man-orchestra approach: traditional South American wooden instruments looped and layered in real time, building a bridge between Andean folklore and the future. The album has since carried Rumbo Tumba to over 30 countries across Latin America, Europe, and Africa. Recording, mixing, and mastering.
